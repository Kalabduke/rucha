Privacy Policy

RUCHA collects what it needs to record your activities and run your account. It does not sell personal data, does not run advertising trackers, and does not read your GPS in the background.

The short version

  • RUCHA uses your location only while you are recording an activity.
  • Your activities are private by default. You choose what to share.
  • We do not sell personal data and we do not run advertising trackers.
  • An account needs a unique username and a password. Social sign-in is only a way to verify you; it does not create a separate profile inside RUCHA.
  • You can use the app without an account, in which case your data stays in this browser on this device.
Plain language. This policy is written to be readable. If a sentence matters to you and is unclear, ask through the public repository and we will explain or correct it.

What we collect

Account data

  • Username and display name. Your username is unique and public to people who search for it. Your display name is shown with your activities and profile.
  • Password. Stored as a one-way bcrypt hash. We never store or log the plain password.
  • Sign-in method. Whether the account uses Telegram, Google, Apple, or a username and password, plus the provider's stable account identifier so the same provider account returns to the same RUCHA account.
  • Consent record. The privacy and terms version you accepted, and the date and time you accepted it.
  • Session data. Random session tokens that keep you signed in. A token is stored in your browser and on the server until you sign out or it expires.

Activity data

  • GPS track: latitude, longitude, elevation, accuracy and timestamps for the points recorded while an activity is running.
  • Activity summary: distance, moving time, pace or speed, climb, calories, title, notes and activity type.
  • Privacy settings: whether an activity is private, friends-only or public, and any share links you create.
  • Social data: friend requests, accepted friendships, challenge participation and results, kudos, and the names of people you share with.
  • Gallery data: run card images you create, their title and summary, and their visibility.

Technical data

Like most web services, the server may keep request logs containing your IP address, browser user agent, the requested path, the time, and error information. These logs are used to keep the service running and to investigate abuse. RUCHA does not build advertising profiles from them.

What we do not collect

  • We do not ask for your phone number or email address to create a RUCHA account.
  • We do not read your contacts, photos, microphone or health records.
  • We do not track your location when no activity is recording.
  • We do not sell personal data or share it with advertising networks.

Location

RUCHA asks for precise location when you start a recording. The browser or operating system shows the permission prompt. If you deny it, recording cannot work, but the rest of the app still opens.

GPS points are attached to the activity you started and stop when you pause, finish or stop the recording. RUCHA does not run a background location service and does not collect location between activities.

Routes reveal places. A start or finish point can reveal a home, workplace or school. Activities are private by default. Review the map before you make a route public or share it with a friend.

If you use RUCHA without an account, the track is stored in this browser's local storage on this device. If you later create an account and accept the offer to move local data, the track is copied to your account.

Why we use it

  • To record, display and store the activities you start.
  • To calculate distance, pace, splits, climb and personal bests.
  • To place you on segment and challenge leaderboards when you take part.
  • To keep you signed in and to protect your account from unauthorized access.
  • To let friends find you by username and to deliver the requests and shares you send.
  • To diagnose errors, prevent abuse, and keep the service available.
  • To record that you accepted the current privacy notice and terms.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or automated decisions that affect your access to the service.

Cookies and local storage

RUCHA does not use advertising cookies or third-party analytics cookies. It uses browser storage for essential functions:

Session tokenKeeps you signed in on this device. Removed when you sign out.

Clearing site data signs you out and removes local-only activities and cards that have not been synced. A signed-in account keeps its data on the server, so clearing the browser does not delete the account.

Who can see it

Your username, display name, profile totals, segment results, challenge standings and any public share are visible to other people who use RUCHA. Your password, session token and sign-in identifiers are never shown to other users.

Activities are private until you change their visibility. A friends-only activity is visible to people you have accepted as friends. A public activity is visible to anyone with the link, including people who are not signed in.

We may disclose data if required by law, or when necessary to investigate fraud, abuse or a serious safety risk. We do not sell personal data. Infrastructure providers process data on our instructions so the service can run.

Sign-in providers

Telegram, Google and Apple verify who you are. When you use one of them, RUCHA receives a stable identifier for that provider account and may receive a display name or an email address. The identifier is stored so the same provider account returns to the same RUCHA account. An email address is used at most to suggest a display name; it is not stored as a contact address and is never used for sign-in.

Telegram sign-in uses the RUCHA bot. The bot receives the chat identifier so it can send the six-digit code. RUCHA does not receive your Telegram password or your phone number through this flow.

These providers have their own privacy policies and may process data on their own terms. Loading Google sign-in, Apple sign-in, Google Fonts or OpenStreetMap tiles makes requests to those services, which receive your IP address and browser information as part of the request.

No email sign-in. RUCHA does not send sign-in links or password-reset email. If you lose your password and cannot use a linked provider, you may need to create a new account.

Retention and deletion

Account data is kept while your account exists. Activity, challenge, friendship and gallery data are kept until you delete them or request account deletion. Server request logs are kept for a limited period for security and troubleshooting.

You can delete individual activities and run cards in the app. To delete an account and its associated data, contact the project through the public repository. Account deletion is currently handled manually; it removes the account, sessions and associated activities, shares and gallery data, and anonymises or removes your profile from friend and challenge views.

Some records may be retained where the law requires it, or where needed to resolve an abuse or security incident. Backups may take a short period to expire after deletion.

Local-only data. If you used RUCHA without an account, clearing site data in your browser deletes the local account, activities and gallery cards. RUCHA cannot recover them.

Your choices

  • Record an activity privately, share it with friends, or publish it with a link.
  • Revoke any share from the account panel.
  • Delete individual activities and run cards.
  • Use RUCHA without an account, keeping data on this device only.
  • Sign out on a shared device.
  • Ask what data is associated with your account, or request deletion, through the public repository.

Some data is necessary for the service to work. If you ask us to delete your account, you will not be able to sign in with that username again unless the name is released.

Security

Passwords are hashed with bcrypt. Sessions use random tokens and can be revoked by signing out. Data in transit should be protected by HTTPS when RUCHA is deployed on its public domain.

No service is completely secure. Use a strong password that you do not reuse elsewhere, do not share your sign-in code with anyone, and sign out on devices you no longer use. If you believe your account is compromised, sign out and contact the project so sessions can be revoked.

Children

RUCHA is a training tool for people who can understand this notice and consent to it. It is not directed at children under 13. If you are under the age of digital consent in your country, use RUCHA only with a parent or guardian who agrees to this policy on your behalf.

If you believe a child created an account without appropriate consent, contact the project and we will review and remove the account.

Changes and contact

RUCHA may update this policy as the app changes. The date and version at the top show the current revision. If a change is material, the app will ask you to review and accept the new version before you continue using your account.

Questions about privacy, or a request to access or delete your data? Open an issue at github.com/Kalabduke/rucha/issues. Issues are public, so do not include passwords, session tokens, sign-in codes or other people's personal data.
