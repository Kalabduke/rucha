Accepting these terms

These Terms of Use are an agreement between you and the people who run RUCHA. By creating an account, signing in, or using the app, you agree to these terms and to the Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, do not use RUCHA.

You must be old enough to enter this agreement in your country. If you are under the age of digital consent where you live, use RUCHA only with a parent or guardian who accepts these terms for you. RUCHA is not directed at children under 13.

One account per person. Do not create accounts to impersonate someone, evade a suspension, manipulate leaderboards, or reserve usernames for other people.

Your account

Unique username and password

Every RUCHA account has a unique username and a password. Usernames are case-insensitive: Selam_Runs and selam_runs are the same name. Choose a username you have the right to use and do not impersonate another person, brand or organization.

Keep your password private. You are responsible for activity on your account while your session is signed in. If you believe someone else has access, sign out, change what you can, and contact the project.

Social sign-in

Telegram, Google and Apple may be used to verify you. The first time you use one of them, you still choose a RUCHA username and password. The provider is a convenience, not a separate account inside RUCHA. If you lose access to a provider, your RUCHA username and password may still work.

Accurate information

Use a display name and profile details that are accurate enough not to mislead other people. You do not have to use your legal name. Do not use another person's name or photo in a way that suggests they are you or that they endorse you.

Account security

Do not share your password, session token or Telegram sign-in code. RUCHA will never ask for your password in a chat, email or issue thread. Report suspected abuse through the public repository.

Acceptable use

Use RUCHA for lawful running, riding, training and community activity. Do not:

  • Record an activity while driving a motor vehicle, riding dangerously, or ignoring traffic laws.
  • Harass, threaten, stalk or impersonate another person.
  • Publish someone else's location, route or personal information without their permission.
  • Upload content that is illegal, hateful, sexually exploitative, or that infringes someone else's rights.
  • Attempt to break into accounts, scrape private data, overload the service, or bypass security controls.
  • Manipulate distance, GPS, segment times, challenge results or leaderboards, including by editing local storage or replaying requests.
  • Use RUCHA to advertise or sell unrelated products without permission.
  • Create accounts in bulk, reserve usernames, or evade a suspension.

We may remove content or restrict an account that breaks these rules. We try to be proportionate, but safety and legal compliance come first.

Your content

You keep ownership of the activities, titles, notes, photos, run cards and other content you create. By saving content in RUCHA, you give RUCHA permission to store it, process it, display it to you, and show it to the people you choose. That permission is limited to operating and improving the service.

If you make an activity or run card public, you understand that anyone with the link can view and share it. If you share with a friend, that person can see it. You are responsible for choosing the right visibility.

Only upload photos and content you have the right to use. A run card may include a photo you took, a photo you have permission to use, or an image you created. Do not upload someone else's copyrighted photo and publish it as your own.

Safety and location

Training is physical activity. Running, cycling and exercising carry a risk of injury, illness and accident. RUCHA is not a coach, a doctor, a safety device or an emergency service. Train within your ability, check the weather and road conditions, wear appropriate gear, and consult a medical professional if you have a health concern.

GPS and elevation data are estimates. They can be wrong, delayed, or affected by buildings, trees, tunnels, weather and the phone's hardware. Do not rely on RUCHA for navigation in an emergency or in an unfamiliar area where a wrong turn could be dangerous.

Your route can reveal where you live, work or train. Activities are private by default. Review the map before you share a route or make it public, and be careful with start and finish points near your home.

Do not interact with the phone while moving. Pull over safely if you need to change a setting, check a map, or read a notification.

Segments and challenges

Segments, challenges and leaderboards are training features. They are not certified races, unless a specific challenge says otherwise. Results depend on GPS accuracy, phone hardware and conditions, and may not be comparable to a watch or an official timing system.

Do not take unsafe risks to improve a time. Do not ride or run against traffic, through red lights, or on closed roads. Respect pedestrians, other road users and the environment.

Prizes, if a challenge offers one, are described on that challenge. RUCHA does not guarantee a prize unless it is stated. We may correct or remove a result that appears to be false, manipulated or unsafe.

Availability and offline data

RUCHA is offered as it is. The server may be offline, the map tiles may be slow, and a browser may lose local data when storage is cleared. The app is designed to keep recording locally when the network is unavailable, but no storage method is permanent.

Keep your own record of important activities if they matter to you. Do not clear site data while you have unsynced local activities. Signing out of a local-only account ends the local session, but the local data remains until you clear it.

We may change, pause or discontinue features. We will try to avoid unnecessary disruption, but we do not promise uninterrupted availability.
