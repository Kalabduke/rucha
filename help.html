How RUCHA works

RUCHA is a GPS training log for runners and cyclists in Ethiopia. Record a route, climb the segments around you, keep a private activity history, and share only what you choose. This guide covers the whole app in a few minutes.

How RUCHA works

RUCHA turns your phone into a simple training computer. You start a recording, put the phone in your pocket or bag, and run or ride. When you finish, RUCHA saves the route, distance, time, pace and climb to your activity log.

1Record

Tap Record and allow precise location. RUCHA reads GPS only while a recording is running.

2Review

Finish the activity to see the map, splits, pace and elevation for the route you covered.

3Keep or share

Everything starts private. Share one activity, open it to friends, or make a run card for social media.

You can use RUCHA without an account. In that case activities, settings and run cards are saved only on this device. Create an account when you want your data tied to a username, friends, challenges and shares across devices.

One account, one person. Every account has a unique username and a password, including accounts created with Telegram, Google or Apple. The username is how friends find you, and the password lets you sign in on any device.

Start here

Create or sign in

Open RUCHA and choose a sign-in method. Telegram is the featured option: you press Start in the RUCHA bot, the bot sends a six-digit code, and you type that code on the site. Google and Apple work the same way as any other social sign-in. After the provider confirms you, RUCHA asks you to choose a username and password once. That pair is your RUCHA account from then on.

  • Username: 3–20 characters, lowercase letters, numbers and underscores only. It is unique and not case sensitive, so Selam_Runs and selam_runs are the same name. The app checks availability as you type.
  • Password: at least 5 characters. Use the eye button to check what you typed. The password is hashed on the server; nobody at RUCHA can read it.
  • Consent: tick the box to accept the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use. Acceptance is stored with your account and the version date is recorded.

Use without an account

Choose Use without an account on the sign-in screen. You still see and accept the privacy notice first. Local mode is useful for a quick run or for testing the app offline. When you later create an account on the same device, RUCHA offers to move your local activities and gallery into that account.

Recording an activity

  1. Open Record from the sidebar, the top bar, or the dashboard.
  2. Choose Run or Ride. The choice changes pace, speed and calorie estimates.
  3. Wait for the GPS pill to show a good accuracy. Outdoors, with a clear view of the sky, is best.
  4. Tap Start. Keep the app open if you want to watch live distance and the route draw itself.
  5. Tap Pause at a traffic light or a rest, then Resume when you move again.
  6. Tap Finish to save the activity. It appears in Activities, on the dashboard map and in your profile totals.
Battery and screens. GPS is the biggest battery user on a phone. A one-hour recording can use a noticeable part of a charge. Start with a full battery for long runs, close other apps, and turn the screen off if you do not need the live map. RUCHA does not record in the background when no activity is running.

Distance goals and auto-stop

On the record screen you can save a distance goal, for example 5 km, and start a goal run. RUCHA stops the timer automatically when you reach the distance. This is useful for a timed effort or a race against a friend.

What the numbers mean

FieldMeaning
DistanceGPS distance along the recorded track, in kilometres.
TimeMoving time. Paused time is not counted.
PaceMinutes per kilometre, shown for runs. Rides use speed.
ElevationTotal climb estimated from the GPS elevation and the terrain.
SplitsTime for each kilometre, visible in the activity detail.

GPS is not a survey instrument. Under trees, between tall buildings and in valleys, the track can wobble. RUCHA filters obvious jumps, but a phone fix can still differ from a watch by a few metres. Treat segments and personal bests as training data, not official race results.

Privacy and sharing

Your activity is private when you save it. No one else sees the route, title or statistics until you choose to share it. There are three visibility levels:

VisibilityWho can see it
PrivateOnly you, on your account.
FriendsPeople you have accepted as friends.
PublicAnyone with the public link you create.

To change visibility or share a single activity, open Activities, choose the activity, and use Share / privacy. The account panel also has a share section, active share links, and a revoke button for every share.

Location is sensitive. A route can reveal where you live, work or train. Check the map before you make an activity public, especially if it starts or ends at home. You can keep the activity private and still make a run card for social media.

Read the full Privacy Policy for what RUCHA stores, why, and how to ask for deletion.

Segments

A segment is a short piece of road or trail with a fixed start and finish. RUCHA includes local climbs, sprints and circuits around Addis Ababa and the surrounding highlands. Open Segments to see the category, distance, average gradient and the current leaderboard.

  • Climb segments reward steady effort on a gradient.
  • Sprint segments are short and fast.
  • Circuits repeat a loop and suit interval training.

When one of your recorded activities passes a segment, RUCHA compares your time with the leaderboard. Segment times depend on GPS accuracy, so a perfect line and a good fix matter more than a fancy watch.

Challenges and goals

Challenges are community goals with a target: distance, time, elevation or a head-to-head route. Open Community to browse open challenges, join one, or create your own. A challenge can be visible to everyone, to friends, or kept private.

Join

Read the target and route, then tap Join. The challenge appears in your community view.

Record

Start a normal recording and choose the challenge. RUCHA checks the route and progress as you move.

Compare

Finishing puts your result on the challenge leaderboard. The standings show time, distance and climb.

Challenges are friendly training tools, not certified races. Do not ride or run dangerously to move up a leaderboard. Follow traffic rules, ride within your ability, and look after the people around you.

Run cards and the gallery

A run card is a shareable image of one activity: your photo in the background, the route drawn on top, and the key numbers. Open Gallery and tap Create a run card.

  1. Choose an activity.
  2. Choose or take a photo. The photo stays on your device while you edit.
  3. Pick a style, adjust the blur and toggle the route.
  4. Share the image directly, download it, or save it to your gallery.

Run cards are saved with your account or in local storage when you use RUCHA without an account. Deleting a card removes it from your gallery; it does not delete the underlying activity.

Post anywhere. A run card is a normal image. RUCHA does not post to your social accounts for you, and it does not add a public location unless you choose a public share.

Your account

Signing in

Use your username and password, or the same Telegram, Google or Apple account you used before. Social sign-in does not skip the RUCHA account: the first time, you still choose a username and password. Later, the provider confirms you and RUCHA signs you into the same account.

Account panel

Tap your avatar in the top bar or the name at the bottom of the sidebar. The panel shows your profile, friends, friend requests, activity sharing, active shares, and links to this guide, the privacy policy and the terms. It also shows which privacy version you accepted and when.

Signing out

Use Sign out in the account panel. On a shared phone, sign out after use. Local-only mode has no password; signing out simply ends the local session.

Friends

Add a friend by their unique username. A request is sent and the other person accepts or declines. Friends can see activities you mark as friends-only, and you can create friends-only challenges.

Offline use and devices

RUCHA is built to keep working when the connection drops. The map falls back to an offline drawing of Addis Ababa, and activities and run cards can be saved on the device. When the server is reachable again, the app syncs what it can.

  • No signal: recording continues because GPS does not need mobile data. The route is saved locally.
  • Slow tiles: switch between the offline map and OpenStreetMap in Explore. The offline map never needs a connection.
  • Local mode: if the RUCHA server is unreachable, you can use the app without an account. Data stays in this browser on this device.
  • Moving to an account: create an account later and accept the offer to move local activities and gallery cards into it.

Sensors

The Record screen can connect to Bluetooth heart-rate, speed/cadence and power sensors where the browser supports it. Sensor support varies by phone and browser. If a sensor does not appear, check that it is awake, not connected to another app, and that Bluetooth permission is allowed for the browser.

Troubleshooting

Location permission was denied

Open your browser's site settings for RUCHA and allow location, then reload the page. On Android, also check that precise location is allowed for the browser. RUCHA cannot record GPS without permission.

The route jumps around

This is usually a weak GPS fix. Move away from tall buildings, wait for a better accuracy before starting, and keep the phone in a pocket or armband with a view of the sky. A phone in a deep bag or under a jacket can lose the signal.

The map is blank or slow

Switch to the offline map in Explore. OpenStreetMap tiles need a connection and can be slow on mobile data. The offline map always works.

My activity did not sync

Check the connection badge. If RUCHA is offline, the activity is saved on this device and will sync when the server is reachable. Do not clear browser storage before it syncs.

I forgot my password

There is no email reset yet. If you still have the Telegram, Google or Apple account linked to RUCHA, sign in that way and you will return to the same RUCHA account. Otherwise, create a new account with a new username. Do not create a second account to impersonate someone else.

A username is taken

Usernames are unique and case-insensitive. Try another combination. The field checks availability as you type, and the server enforces uniqueness when you submit.

I cannot sign in with Apple

Apple sign-in requires an HTTPS site and Apple credentials configured on the server. If the button is disabled or missing, use Telegram, Google, or a username and password.

Questions

Is RUCHA free?

Yes. Recording, segments, challenges and run cards are free to use. There are no ads and RUCHA does not sell personal data.

Does RUCHA track me in the background?

No. GPS is read only while an activity is recording. When you finish or stop, tracking stops. The app does not build a background location history.

Can two people have the same username?

No. Usernames are unique and case-insensitive. Selam_Runs and selam_runs are the same account name.

Can I use RUCHA on more than one device?

Yes, with an account. Sign in with the same username and password on each device. Local-only mode is tied to the browser and device where it was created.

Who can see my route?

Only you, until you share the activity or change its visibility. Public shares are visible to anyone with the link. Friends-only shares are visible to accepted friends.

How do I delete my data?

Delete individual activities or run cards in the app. For account deletion, contact the project through the public repository. See the Privacy Policy for what deletion covers.

Found a bug or something unclear? Open an issue at github.com/Kalabduke/rucha/issues. Include your phone, browser and what you expected to happen. Never post your password or sign-in code.
